**中文**

一期有两条缺口挂着从未被仪器测量过的「重启条件」——PTC 等批量步的收益标签，去重等重复率。没有分母，条件永远无法满足。这一版把两个分母量出来，并据此改变二期路线。

- 新增 `continuation`：整步绑定（上一步的所有字符串参数都逐字来自上一步回执）——951/54,141 步，占 1.47% token。这是 PTC 式合并往返的收益天花板。
- 新增 `duplicates`：重复调用率 150/62,624 = 0.24%（同一步 2、跨步 148）。去重从「无法计算」变成「可计算且很小」。
- 修正一处我自己的口径错误：第一版把「一步里有几个调用」当成「能折叠几次往返」，算出 14.11%——多调用步本来就在同一次往返里，那个数什么都不测。
- 结论写进文档：PTC 的重启条件改为「上限 1.47% + 实测收益 + 质量标签」，去重按现有证据不值得做。

**English**

Phase one carried two "restart conditions" that no instrument had ever measured — PTC waiting on a batch-step benefit label, deduplication waiting on a duplicate rate. Without a denominator neither condition could ever be met. This release measures both, and the phase-two roadmap changes as a result.

- New `continuation` metric: whole-step binding (every string argument of the next step comes verbatim from the previous receipt) — 951/54,141 steps, 1.47% of tokens. That is the ceiling for PTC-style round-trip folding.
- New `duplicates` metric: 150/62,624 = 0.24% (2 within-step, 148 cross-step). Deduplication moves from "uncomputable" to "computable and small".
- Corrects a denominator error of mine: the first version counted "calls per step" as "round trips that could be folded", producing 14.11% — multi-call steps already share one round trip, so that number measured nothing.
- Written into the docs: PTC's restart condition is now "a 1.47% ceiling plus measured benefit plus a quality label"; deduplication is not worth building on current evidence.
